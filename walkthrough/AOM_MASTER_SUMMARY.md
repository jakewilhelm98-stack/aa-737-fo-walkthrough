## Memorized Limitations

Per AOM 18.1.1, limitations marked in bold italic in the AOM "must be memorized." These are the items where reference cannot assure timely compliance. The exact wording need not be memorized — only enough to ensure compliance.

### Autoflight
- **Minimum height for autopilot use after takeoff: 400 ft AGL**
- **Minimum height for single-channel autopilot use during approach: 50 ft AGL**
- **Use of aileron trim with the autopilot engaged is prohibited**
- **Do not use LVL CHG on final approach below 1000 ft AFE**

### Altitude / Speed
- **Maximum operating altitude with flaps and/or slats extended: 20,000 ft**
- **Severe turbulence air penetration airspeed: 280 kt / .76 Mach**

### APU
- **APU bleed + electrical load: max altitude 10,000 ft**
- **APU bleed: max altitude 17,000 ft**
- **APU electrical load: max altitude 41,000 ft**

### Flight Controls / Speedbrakes
- **Holding in icing conditions with flaps extended is prohibited**
- **Avoid rapid and large alternating control inputs, especially in combination with large pitch/roll/yaw changes — may cause structural failure even below VA**
- **Do not deploy speedbrakes in flight at radio altitudes less than 1000 ft AFL**
- **3RA-3VM (MAX 8): do not extend speedbrake lever beyond ARMED detent with flaps 40 selected**

### Engines
- **Intentional selection of reverse thrust in flight is prohibited**

### Communications
- **Do not operate HF radios during refueling operations**

### Weather Radar
- **Avoid weather radar operations in a hangar** (test mode is the exception)
- Avoid radar operation when personnel are within the area normally enclosed by the nose radome

### Doors
- **Door wind limits:** Do not operate entry/service doors in winds > 40 kt; do not let doors stay open in gusts > 65 kt

## Ch 18 — Limitations

### 18.2.1 Altitude Limits
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum operating altitude | 41,000 ft |
| Maximum takeoff and landing altitude | 8,400 ft |
| Maximum operating altitude with flaps/slats extended | **20,000 ft** |

**RVSM altimeter tolerances:**
- In-flight CA vs FO max difference: 200 ft
- On-ground CA vs FO: 50 ft (SL–5,000 ft); 60 ft (5,001–10,000 ft)
- On-ground CA or FO vs field elevation: 75 ft
- Standby altimeter does not meet RVSM accuracy

### 18.2.2 Speed Limits
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum operating airspeed (VMO) | 340 kt |
| Maximum operating Mach (MMO) | .82 M |
| Severe turbulence penetration | **280 kt / .76 M** |
| Max gear extension speed (VLO) | 270 kt / .82 M |
| Max gear extended speed (VLE) | 320 kt / .82 M |
| Max gear retraction speed (VLO) | 235 kt |

**Maximum Flap Operating Speeds:**
| Flaps | Speed |
|---|---|
| 1 | 240 |
| 2 | 240 |
| 5 | 240 |
| 10 | 210 |
| 15 | 200 |
| 25 | 190 |
| 30 | 175 |
| 40 (NG / MAX) | 162 / 166 |

VA (design maneuvering) is shown on the VMO/MMO charts; not displayed on the PFD. Turbulence penetration speed is a rough proxy for VA. Even below VA, avoid full or abrupt control movements.

### 18.2.3 Weight Limits
| Weight (lbs) | -NG (3AA-3PX) | -MAX 8 (3RA-3VM) |
|---|---|---|
| Max taxi | 174,700 | 182,700 |
| Max takeoff | 174,200 | 182,200 |
| Max landing | 146,300 | 152,800 |
| Max zero fuel | 138,300 | 145,400 |

Refer to QRH Overweight Landing procedure and FOM for non-normal/emergency landing.

### 18.2.4 Wind Limits
All winds are based on peak gust value.

| Item | kt |
|---|---|
| Max wind for takeoff and landing | 50 |
| Max tailwind for takeoff | 15 (special data may be required) |
| Max tailwind for landing | 10 |
| Max headwind CAT II/III | 25 |
| HUD TO tailwind | 10 |

**Takeoff crosswind limits (peak gust):**
- Dry: 33 (NG) / 32 (MAX)
- Wet: 25
- Standing water/slush > 1/8 in: 15
- Snow (wet/dry): 25
- Compacted snow: 20
- Ice: 15
- Narrow runway (< 148 ft / 45 m): 20
- HUD TO: 10

**Landing crosswind limits (RCC PIREP):**
- 6 / Dry: 33 (NG) / 32 (MAX)
- 5 Good: 33 / 32
- 4 Good-Medium / 3 Medium: 25
- 2 Medium-Poor: 17
- 1 Poor: 15
- HUD SA CAT I / CAT II/III: 15
- Vis < 3/4 SM (RVR 4000 / 1200 m): 15
- Narrow runway (< 148 ft / 45 m): 20

Reduce crosswind limits by 5 kt on wet/contaminated runways whenever asymmetric reverse thrust is used.

**Ground wind limits:**
- > 43 kt crosswind: limit thrust to taxi setting
- > 58 kt: idle thrust except setting takeoff thrust on the runway
- Doors: do not operate > 40 kt; do not leave open in gusts > 65 kt

### 18.2.5 Miscellaneous
- Minimum flight crew: Captain + First Officer
- Maximum runway slope: ±2.0% (uphill / downhill)

### 18.3 Aircraft General
- Overwater seating: NG up to 181 seats, MAX up to 182 seats; life raft capacity 193
- Min one floor-level exit must be open or armed any time pax are on board prior to movement
- Escape slide girt bar must be inserted in brackets on all doors during taxi/takeoff/landing
- Flightdeck door access system test is required prior to the first flight of the day
- Live animals only in the FORWARD cargo compartment

**Minimum Crew Oxygen Pressure for Dispatch (psi):**
| Bottle Temp °C | 2 crew | 3 crew | 4 crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 530 | 735 | 945 |
| 30 | 495 | 690 | 885 |
| 10 | 460 | 645 | 830 |
| -10 | 430 | 600 | 770 |

### 18.4 Air Systems
- With either or both engine BLEED switches ON: do not operate packs in HIGH for takeoff/approach/landing (exceptions: CARGO FIRE, SMOKE/FUMES REMOVAL, VOLATILE LIQUID checklists)
- Cargo: live animals forward compartment only

### 18.4.2 Pressurization
- Max altitude for unpressurized flight after inflight depressurization: 14,000 ft (may exceed for terrain)
- Max altitude when dispatched for unpressurized flight: 10,000 ft
- Max cabin pressure differential (safety relief setting): 9.1 psi

### 18.5 Anti-Ice / Rain
**Definition of icing conditions:**
- OAT (ground / takeoff) or TAT (inflight) ≤ 10°C, AND
- Visible moisture present (clouds, fog ≤ 1 mile vis, rain, snow, sleet, ice crystals), OR
- Ramps/taxiways/runways with surface snow/ice/standing water/slush

**Engine anti-ice:**
- Required ON for all ground/flight operations when icing conditions exist or are anticipated
- Exception: climb and cruise when SAT < -40°C
- Required ON prior to and during descent in icing, including SAT < -40°C
- Engine ignition ON when operating in icing
- Do NOT use when OAT (ground) or TAT (flight) > 10°C
- 3RA-3VM (MAX): do not operate EAI in flight when not in actual or anticipated icing (engine inlet damage risk)

**Wing anti-ice:**
- Not a substitute for ground deice/anti-ice
- Do not operate on the ground when OAT > 10°C
- **Holding in icing conditions with flaps extended is prohibited** (memory item)

### 18.6 Autoflight
- **LVL CHG: do not use on final approach below 1000 ft AFE** (memory)
- **Aileron trim with autopilot engaged is prohibited** (memory)
- Dual channel autopilot for approach is prohibited
- **Min AP after takeoff: 400 ft AGL** (memory)
- **Min AP single channel on approach: 50 ft AGL** (memory)

### 18.7 APU
- After three consecutive aborted starts: 15-minute cooling period required
- **APU bleed + electrical: max 10,000 ft** (memory)
- **APU bleed only: max 17,000 ft** (memory)
- **APU electrical only: max 41,000 ft** (memory)
- APU bleed valve must be CLOSED when:
  - Ground air connected and isolation valve open
  - Engine 1 bleed valve open
  - Isolation valve and engine 2 bleed valves both open
- APU bleed may be open during engine start, but avoid engine power above idle

### 18.8 Communications
- **HF radios: do not operate during refueling** (memory)
- If one HF selected for transmission, deselect the other on all audio panels
- BROADBAND SYSTEM switch OFF during deicing, in a hangar, or stopped under a skybridge (mask deploy switch is right next to it)

### 18.9 Electrical
- Pulling and resetting circuit breakers is not authorized unless specified in a procedure (see 16.6)

### 18.10 Engines
- NG: CFM56-7B26K
- MAX 8: CFM LEAP-1B

**Engine ignition required:**
- Takeoff: AUTO or CONT
- Landing: AUTO or CONT
- Anti-ice: AUTO or CONT
- Heavy rain: CONT

**Starter duty cycle:**
- NG: 2 min ON, 10 s OFF
- MAX: 3 min ON, 10 s OFF

**Extended motoring (NG / MAX):**
- NG first 2 motorings: 15 min ON / 2 min OFF; third+: 5 min ON / 10 min OFF
- MAX first 2 motorings: 5 min ON / 5 min OFF; third+: 10 min OFF

**Oil:**
- MAX 8: ensure oil temp ≥ 31°C before setting takeoff thrust (cold-soak idle warm-up table provided in AOM)
- Min oil for dispatch: NG 12 qt, MAX 14 qt

**Reverse thrust:**
- Ground operations only
- **Intentional selection in flight prohibited** (memory)
- Backing the aircraft with reverse is prohibited
- Do not attempt go-around after thrust reverser deployment on landing

**Thrust limits / EEC alternate mode:**
- Antiskid INOP: assumed-temperature reduced thrust takeoff is NOT permitted
- EEC ALT mode takeoff: both EECs in alt; NG max 26K (27K if planned); MAX use TO thrust; don't use FMC TO N1 or QRH V-speeds; autothrottle takeoff is prohibited
- 27K Bump (NG): only authorized if specified on TPS by load agent; no ACARS request if TPS based on other rating; KSNA exception

### 18.11 Flight Controls
- See altitude limits and max flap speeds
- **Holding in icing with flaps extended is prohibited** (memory)
- Alternate flap duty cycle: 15 sec wait between switch movements; after a complete extend/retract cycle (e.g., 0→15→0), 5 minute cooling
- **Avoid rapid and large alternating control inputs** (structural failure risk even below VA)
- **Do not deploy speedbrakes in flight below 1000 ft AFL** (memory)
- In flight, do not extend speedbrake lever beyond FLIGHT detent
- **MAX 8: do not extend speedbrake lever beyond ARMED detent with flaps 40 selected** (memory)

### 18.12 Flight Instruments / Displays
- HUD use with INOP #1 RA is prohibited
- ISFD must be initialized per approved procedures

### 18.13 Flight Management / Navigation
- GPS updating must be disabled for approach when operating outside US National Airspace if FMC database/charts are not WGS-84 (China and Russia)
- IRS alignment NOT to be attempted at latitudes > 78°15' N
- Magnetic heading/track ops prohibited where IRS Mag Var table errors > 5°
- Max flight ops latitude depends on ADIRU mag var tables (82° N/S; with regional restrictions in West/Central Pacific area)
- Radio altimeter 5G AD restrictions: AA approved AMOC; restrictions do not apply

### 18.14 Fuel
- Approved standard: Jet A, Jet A-1; alternates: JP-5, JP-8; prohibited: JP-4, Jet B
- Max tank fuel temp: 49°C
- Tank fuel temp prior to takeoff: ≥ -43°C OR fuel freeze point + 3°C, whichever higher
- Fuel system icing inhibitor does NOT change min temperature limit
- **Fuel loading rule:** If center tank > 1000 lbs, mains 1 and 2 must be FULL
- Ballast fuel not authorized
- Intentional running of a center tank pump with low-pressure light illuminated is PROHIBITED
- **Lateral imbalance between mains:** scheduled to zero; max 1,000 lbs at taxi, takeoff, in-flight, or landing
- 3RA-3VM (MAX): operation with Kathon FP 1.5 biocide prohibited (no crew action required — fueling manual handles)

### 18.15 Landing Gear / Brakes
- See 18.2.2 Speed Limits for VLO/VLE
- Do NOT apply brakes until after touchdown

### 18.16 Warning Systems
**EGPWS:**
- Navigation must NOT be predicated on the Terrain Display
- Pilots authorized to deviate from ATC clearance to comply with an EGPWS warning
- Inhibit EGPWS enhanced function < 15 NM from airfield when:
  - Operating to/from runways not in EGPWS database (see 21p.1.3 procedure)
  - Specific approaches identified as causing false terrain alerts

**3UN-3VM ORW (Overrun Warning):**
- Does NOT replace landing performance assessment requirement
- Lack of overrun alert is NOT a guarantee of stopping margin
- In-air "OVERRUN, GO-AROUND" → execute immediate go-around
- INHIBIT ORW when: ground OAT < -40°C or > 50°C; or GW > max landing weight

**3UN-3VM RAAS (Runway Awareness):**
- Do not use for real-time runway suitability or performance
- Do not use callouts for navigation
- Not a substitute for NOTAMs or ATIS

## Ch 3 — Preflight

### 3.2 Electrical Power Up

**3.2.1 Procedure (common path, all branches):**
1. BAT switch — ON, guard closed (Boeing "Guarded" = switch ON, guard closed). 30 sec wait between BAT guard close and APU start if no external power (DPC self-test).
2. STANDBY POWER switch — Guarded
3. ALTERNATE FLAPS switch — Guarded
4. L and R WIPER selectors — PARK
5. ELEC1 and ELEC2 HYD PUMPS — OFF
6. LANDING GEAR lever — DN (3 green, 3 red extg)

**Branch A — External power available:**
7. GRD POWER AVAILABLE light — Illum
   - GRD PWR switch — ON; verify SOURCE OFF, TRANSFER BUS OFF, STANDBY PWR OFF lights extinguished
   - Then accomplish General Preflight or Origination Flow as applicable

**Branch B — No ext power, APU electrics available:**
7. ENG 1, ENG 2, APU FIRE switches — IN
8. Ground personnel — ALERT (APU ground panel horn will fire during test)
9. Overheat and Fire Protection panel — Check (21g.1.1 Fire & Overheat System Test)
10. APU — Start (21c.1.1)
11. Electrical Power / Air Conditioning — Establish (3.2.2)
12. 3AA-3MX Wheel Well Fire Detection System — Test

**Branch C — No ext power AND no APU electrics:**
- 3AA-3PX: Battery Start with APU bleed or ground air (21f.1.3)
- 3RA-3VM: Battery Start lives in maintenance manual; mx personnel required

### 3.2.2 Power / Air Conditioning Priorities
- Electrical: external power preferred to reduce APU
- Air conditioning: PCA preferred; APU only when PCA inop or pax comfort needs
- Hard rule: do NOT operate packs when PCA is connected (damages pack components)

### 3.3 Exterior Inspection
- Normally accomplished by FO before every flight
- 19 inspection stations starting at left forward fuselage, proceeding clockwise
- Always verify: gear pins removed, chocks placed, no fluid leaks beyond engine drain limits, fan cowl latches, RVSM area undamaged, probes/static ports/AOA vanes clear, tires/wheels/struts within limits
- Engine drain leak rule: continuous stream = mx required, do not start

### Origination & General Preflight Flows
The flightdeck flows are detailed in 3f. Origination is first-flight-of-day or after unobserved mx; General Preflight is every flight. Both require Electrical Power Up be completed FIRST.

## Ch 4 — Pushback / Start

**Sequence:** Beacon ON → cleared to start → pushback comm → start engines → after-start flows (CA + FO) → "I have a salute" → flight control check → recall check → After Start checklist.

**Key items:**
- Engine start initiation: STAB (status above ~25%), N1 motoring, ignition automatic via auto-start
- Engine start abort: dry motor for 30 sec then per QRH if hot/hung start
- Recall Check (after both engines stable, before checklist): press either Master Caution annunciator panel; both Master Caution lights illuminate, both 6-packs fully lit. Faults that reset = single fault dispatchable per MEL; faults that remain = dual fault, NNC required
- Flight control check: full deflection all axes, both pilots verify (single-engine taxi: after 2nd engine start)
- Anti-ice configuration set per OAT/visible moisture criteria (Ch 18.5)

## Ch 5 — Taxi

**Standard taxi:**
- Beacon ON, packs as set, anti-ice as required
- Speed: not more than reasonable for conditions; straight ahead first to verify brakes
- Steering: tiller for tight turns; rudder pedals for gentle
- Stop rules: do not roll continuously through stop bars
- Single-engine taxi rules: see 5.x; flight control check completes after 2nd engine start before takeoff

**Crosswind / contaminated taxi:**
- Reverse and braking discipline; minimize control inputs; expect reduced friction

## Ch 6 — Takeoff

### 6.1 Normal Takeoff (Two Engine)
- Thrust set: TO/GA pressed; verify N1 stabilizes at target; "Thrust set ___"
- 80 kt callout; airspeed agreement check
- V1 (if required by procedure)
- VR — rotate 2-3°/sec to ~15° pitch
- Positive rate → Gear up
- Above 400 ft RA: verify roll mode; LNAV auto-engages if armed
- ACCEL HT: VNAV (or LVL CHG, set speed)
- Flap retraction on schedule
- "After takeoff checklist"

**Reduced thrust (assumed temperature):**
- Not permitted with antiskid INOP
- Not permitted with EECs in ALT mode (NG: 26K max, 27K if planned)
- 27K Bump only when load agent TPS specifies it

### 6.2 Rejected Takeoff (RTO)
- Below V1: Captain has SOLE authority to reject
- At/above V1: GO unless airplane cannot fly
- RTO actions: thrust IDLE, max reverse, max braking, monitor speedbrake deploy
- PM: "Deployed" / "No speedbrakes" / monitors deceleration
- After stop: PM runs Rejected Takeoff checklist

### 6.3 HUD Low-Visibility Takeoff
- HUD AIII (or AII/IMC) required per visibility published
- HUD takeoff tailwind: max 10 kt
- HUD takeoff crosswind: max 10 kt

### 6.4 Loss of Thrust at or Above V1
- Continue takeoff; rotate at VR; maintain V2 (or V2+ENG OUT speed); positive rate gear up
- Engine-out SID lateral path if published
- Above 400 ft (or per ##-7E): verify roll mode
- At/above 1000 ft AFL (or per ##-7E): "LVL CHG, set speed" → flap retraction → "MCT, ____ checklist"

### Special Considerations
- Engine anti-ice ON before takeoff in icing
- Wing anti-ice OFF for takeoff unless required by procedure
- Anti-ice and ignition rules per Ch 18

## Ch 10 — Approach

### 10.1 General
- 10.1.1 Initial Approach Actions & Callouts (entry into approach segment)
- 10.1.2 Authorized approaches: ILS (CAT I/II/III, SA CAT I/II), RNAV (GPS), RNAV(RNP)/RNP(AR), LDA/DME, LOC, NDB, VOR
- 10.1.3 Aircraft category (NG SFP = Cat C; NG and MAX = Cat D)
- Glideslope from above technique: V/S -1000 to -1500 fpm, stable by 1000 AFL
- HUD modes per flight operation: NP/PRI for visual/non-ILS/CAT I, AII/AIII/IMC for low vis

### 10.2 Stabilized Approach (FRAMEWORK)
**Required by 1000 ft AFL (IMC and VMC):**
- Landing configuration (gear down, final landing flaps)
- Descent rate ≤ 1000 fpm

**Required by 1000 ft AFL IMC (or 500 ft AFL VMC if delayed and called):**
- On approach speed (target -5 / +10 kt)
- On flight path
- Stabilized (spooled) thrust

**Descent rate limits:**
- Below 2000 ft AFL: ≤ 2000 fpm
- Below 1000 ft AFL or inside FAF: ≤ 1000 fpm

**Deviation callouts:**
- Airspeed (with landing flaps): "Airspeed" if < target -5 or > target +10
- "Sink rate" for descent rate exceedances
- ILS: "Localizer" (1/3 dot or expanded scale diamond touches box), "Glideslope" (1/2 dot after capture)
- Non-ILS: "Path" (vertical), "Track" (cross-track), "VOR" (>1 dot bearing)
- VOR/LOC in LNAV: use "Track"

**Either pilot may direct a go-around.** Decision to GA is good judgment, NOT poor performance. Directed GA must be executed unless emergency overrides.

### 10.3 ILS General
- Set MCP altitude to MAA after G/S capture, NLT 1000 ft AFL
- MINS reference: BARO for CAT I, SA CAT I; RADIO for CAT II/III
- VHF NAV 1 and 2 set to ILS frequency
- Both MCP courses inbound
- Watch for false LOC captures; uncouple, HDG SEL to intercept, re-arm APP
- Glideslope capture inhibited until LOC captured

### 10.4 ILS CAT I
- DA based on barometric altitude
- AP recommended; required OFF NLT 50 ft AGL
- < 4000 RVR or 3/4 SM: autobrakes required, AP must be OFF, crosswind max 15 kt
- Tailwind max 10 kt
- Cat D minimums
- SA CAT I (HUD-equipped, CAT II currency, CA flies): DH as low as 150 ft, RVR 1400; AP authorized to 50 ft AGL

### 10.5 ILS SA CAT II / CAT II / III
- Captain assumes PF duties prior to approach
- HUD required to touchdown
- Actions and callouts identical for CAT II and III down to DH
- AP disconnected NLT 1000 ft AFL (different from CAT I)
- AIII required below RVR 1800; AII/IMC may be used down to RVR 1200 if AIII unavailable
- Crosswind max 15 kt; headwind max 25 kt; tailwind max 10 kt
- Must have a usable LOC inside the runway threshold
- Any HUD/HAP amber or red caution/warning on FAS while IMC = mandatory go-around
- LOC or G/S full deflection (≥ 2.5 dots, hollow diamond) anytime = go-around

### 10.6 Non-ILS (RNAV/RNP, LNAV/VNAV, LOC, VOR, NDB)
- AP REQUIRED with ceiling < 1000 ft AGL and/or vis < 3 SM (1000-3)
- AP required for RNP(AR) below RNP 0.3
- RNAV(RNP)/RNP(AR) NOT authorized outside BARO-VNAV temperature limits
- VNAV PTH descent to DA, V/S to DDA
- Min RNP authorized: 0.1
- VNAV at DA: begin climb immediately, continue along final approach to MAP, do NOT initiate published miss before MAP

### 10.7 Visual Approach
- 2.5° to 3° final path
- ~300 ft per NM gives normal profile
- Stable by ~500 ft above touchdown
- RoD on final 700-900 fpm; > 1000 fpm should be avoided
- Flaps NOT used as drag — speedbrakes first, then early gear extension
- RNAV Visual / FMS-CVFP must be retrievable from FMS database (crew not authorized to build)

### 10.8 Circling Maneuver
- Aircraft remains clear of clouds; ceiling ≥ 1000 ft, vis ≥ 3 SM (or charted, whichever higher)
- MDA = 1000 ft AFL or charted MDA (whichever higher), rounded UP to nearest 100 ft
- Min config: gear down, flaps 15, speedbrake armed
- Stay at/above MDA until TDZ in sight AND normal descent/turn will land in proper TDZ
- Loss of visual reference: climbing turn TOWARD landing runway, then fly published miss

## Ch 11 — Go-Around

### 11.1 Normal Go-Around
- Either pilot may make the GA callout; PF MUST execute directed GA unless emergency overrides
- From flaps 30/40 → "Flaps 15"
- From flaps 15 → "Flaps 1" (limit bank to 15° when airspeed < VREF15+15 or min maneuver, whichever lower)
- TO/GA push: A/T engages GA; below 2000 ft RA in GA mode (1000-2000 fpm climb); above 2000 ft RA with G/S engaged or flaps down → N1 mode (full GA N1)
- Second TO/GA push for full GA thrust if needed
- Standard takeoff (assumed temp): GA mode may not advance to full GA if cruise alt below ~15,000 ft
- Above 400 ft RA: roll mode (HDG SEL or LNAV); LNAV auto-engages above 400 ft RA
- Flap retraction on schedule with maneuver speeds (speed window stays closed)

### 11.2 Engine-Out Go-Around
- Push TO/GA, advance thrust lever(s) to GA thrust, "Flaps 1"
- Rotate to ~13° pitch
- Yaw control: rudder + trim
- Above 400 ft RA: extended runway centerline OR EO SID departure path
- Min 1000 ft AFL (or per ##-7E): "LVL CHG, set speed" → flap retraction → "MCT, ____ checklist"
- Maintain Vapp until 1000 ft AFL minimum; bank ≤ 15° until clean and safe

### 11.3 Rejected Landing
- **WARNING: After reverse thrust is initiated, full stop landing MUST be made**
- Before TD with TD occurring: continue normal GA; FD GA mode still provides guidance
- After TD before reversers: smoothly advance to GA, MAINTAIN landing flap config, verify speedbrakes retracted, autobrakes disarm; rotate at VREF
- Takeoff config warning may sound momentarily if flaps not at 15

### 11.4 PRM Breakouts
- ATC "Traffic alert" → "Breakout"
- Disengage AP/AT simultaneously
- Climbing breakout: turn + climb as directed
- Descending breakout: turn + descend as directed, max 1000 fpm
- Follow TCAS RA vertical guidance if received (overrides ATC vertical, follow ATC lateral)
- If LOC/GS captured: cycle both FDs OFF then ON
- Set up MCP, then "Set speed ___"

## Ch 12 — Landing

### 12.1 Normal Landing (Touchdown to Stop)
**Touchdown:**
- Verify thrust levers closed (caution: low energy + idle = hard landing/tailstrike)
- Verify SPEEDBRAKE FULL UP — "Deployed" / "No speedbrakes" if failure
- Monitor autobrakes
- Reverse thrust to interlocks, then as needed (warning: reverse = committed to stop)
- "Single reverse" / "No reverse" if abnormal — caution applying reverse on remaining engine

**Ground roll:**
- "80." (PM)
- Reduce reverse to be at IDLE reverse by 60
- "60." (PM)
- After engines reverse idle: full down on reverse levers
- Transition to manual braking — "Manual braking"
- "Autobrakes off" if AUTO BRAKE DISARM light
- Verify REV indication extinguished; PM watches for forward thrust during cancel
- FO landing: "My aircraft" / "Your aircraft" handoff to CA at taxi speed

### 12.1.3 Flare and Touchdown
- Cross threshold ~50 ft → 1000 ft TDZ touchdown point
- Wind additives held to touchdown
- Body attitudes vary by airframe / flap setting / weight (see AOM tables)
- Attitudes reduced 1°/5 kt above VREF+5
- Tail clearance reduces with high pitch attitude

### 12.2 Crosswind Landing
Three accepted methods:
1. **De-crab during flare:** wings level on final, kick out crab in flare with downwind rudder, control roll with into-wind aileron
2. **Touchdown in crab:** zero sideslip; recommended for very slippery runways but not on dry with strong crosswind
3. **Sideslip (wing-low):** opposite rudder + lower upwind wing
Combinations for strong crosswinds.

### 12.3 Landing Irregularities (Bounced Landing)
- Shallow bounce/skip: hold or re-establish normal landing attitude; thrust may be required
- High hard bounce: GO AROUND
- Manually advance thrust to GA; verify speedbrakes retracted
- Do NOT retract flaps/gear until positive rate (second touchdown may occur)
- Hard landing: AML entry, captain final authority on classification

### 12.4 Brake Energy / Use
- Do not apply brakes until after touchdown
- Autobrakes per planned setting; manual braking as needed during ground roll

## Ch 17 — Inflight Maneuvers

### 17.1 Approach to Stall / Stall Recovery
- Recover at FIRST indication (stickshaker OR initial buffet) — same procedure for impending or full stall
- "My aircraft" → AP/AT off → nose-down elevator (full forward may be required) → nose-down trim if needed
- Reduction of AoA is the SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT action
- High thrust + low airspeed: REDUCING thrust may aid pitch (underwing engines pitch up with thrust)
- Roll wings level only after AoA reduced
- Do NOT change gear/flap config (exception: at liftoff with flaps up → flaps 1)
- Do NOT use FD commands during recovery

### 17.2 Upset Recovery
**Upset definition:**
- Pitch > 25° nose up
- Pitch > 10° nose down
- Bank > 45°
- Less than above but inappropriate airspeed for conditions

**Nose High Recovery:**
- "My aircraft" → AP/AT off → nose-down elevator → nose-down trim → REDUCE thrust → roll (up to ~60°) to obtain nose-down pitch rate
- Approaching horizon: roll wings level

**Nose Low Recovery:**
- "My aircraft" → AP/AT off → recover from stall first if stalled → roll wings level (shortest direction; if bank > 90°, UNLOAD then roll)
- Speedbrakes as needed for high airspeed
- When airspeed sufficiently decreasing: nose-up elevator + nose-up trim if needed

**Notes:**
- Excessive trim or rudder can aggravate the upset
- Higher than normal control forces may be required
- HUD/HGS displays upset recovery symbology
- Use ADI as primary attitude reference

### 17.3 Windshear Escape Maneuver
- "Escape" / "My aircraft"
- Disengage AP, push TO/GA, MAX thrust (full forward; firewall if terrain imminent), disengage AT
- Simultaneously roll wings level + rotate to 15° initial pitch
- Retract speedbrakes
- Follow FD TO/GA, do NOT exceed PLI (intermittent shaker = upper pitch limit)
- Do NOT change gear/flap config or attempt to regain lost airspeed until windshear no longer a factor

**Alerts:**
- Caution "Monitor radar display" → continue if able to avoid
- Warning "Windshear ahead" → normal GA OR escape
- Warning "Windshear, windshear, windshear" → ESCAPE maneuver
- Inhibit ranges: caution 80 kt to 400 ft RA; warning 100 kt to 50 ft RA

### 17.4 EGPWS
**Caution alerts:** AIRSPEED LOW, CAUTION TERRAIN/OBSTACLE, SINK RATE, TERRAIN, DON'T SINK, TOO LOW FLAPS/GEAR/TERRAIN, GLIDESLOPE, BANK ANGLE
- Correct flight path/configuration/airspeed; if can't meet stable criteria → go around

**Warning alerts:** PULL UP, TERRAIN TERRAIN PULL UP, OBSTACLE OBSTACLE PULL UP, unacceptable flight toward terrain
- "My aircraft" → AP/AT off → MAX thrust → simultaneously wings level + rotate to 20° initial pitch (NOT 15°)
- Retract speedbrakes
- If terrain threat remains, continue rotation to PLI / stickshaker / initial buffet
- Do NOT alter gear/flap config until terrain clearance assured
- Do NOT use FD commands

### 17.5 TCAS
**TA:** Look for traffic, call out conflicts. Do NOT maneuver based on TA alone.

**RA:** Respond IMMEDIATELY even if conflicts with ATC clearance.
- Standard RA: AP/AT off, smoothly adjust pitch and thrust, follow lateral flight path
- **DESCEND RA below 1000 ft AGL must NOT be followed**
- Once RA issued, do NOT change vertical speed except to comply
- Controller vertical conflicts with RA → follow RA vertical, comply with controller lateral
- Climb RA in landing configuration: AP/AT off → MAX thrust → "Flaps 15" → smoothly satisfy RA → "Positive rate" → "Gear up"

### 17.6 Emergency Descent
- "Emergency Descent" / "My aircraft"
- Set MCP lower (10,000 ft or MSA, whichever higher); LVL CHG; close thrust levers; speedbrakes FLIGHT DETENT; set MMO/VMO
- PM: PA "Emergency descent, use oxygen, fasten seat belts"; FASTEN BELTS ON; START switches CONT; notify ATC; check safe altitude
- Callouts: "2000 above level off" / "1000 above level off" / speedbrakes RETRACT
- Normally gear UP; gear extended only if structural integrity in doubt

### 17.7 Driftdown / One Engine Cruise
- AT off → MCT on operating engine → MCP IAS/MACH = ENG OUT driftdown speed → MCP ALT = max ENG OUT alt
- After decel: LVL CHG
- Refer to QRH ENGINES, APU > Driftdown and One Engine Cruise
- Long Overwater: .79/335 KIAS, One Engine High Speed Cruise chart

### 17.9 Overspeed (VMO/MMO Exceedance)
- Leave AP engaged unless clearly not correcting
- Manual: smoothly disengage AP, do NOT reduce thrust to idle at altitude (slow re-accel)
- Document any exceedance in AML
- Speedbrake retraction near VMO/MMO can momentarily overspeed — retract slowly

### 17.10 Aircraft Aerodynamic Characteristics
- Lateral and directional control: ailerons primary, rudder secondary
- Underwing engines have nose-up pitch moment with thrust (relevant to upset and stall recovery)
- Speedbrakes: increase initial buffet/shaker speed but lesser effect on actual stall

## Memory Items vs Memorized Limitations

**Important distinction:**

- **AOM 18.1.1 "Memorized Limitations"** — These are limitations marked in bold italic in Chapter 18 that pilots must know by heart because reference cannot assure timely compliance. These are the items listed at the top of this document. They are LIMITATIONS, not procedures.

- **QRH "Memory Items" / "Reference Items"** — These are the recall (memory) and immediate action items for non-normal procedures (e.g., the initial actions for cabin altitude warning, engine fire/severe damage/separation, runaway stabilizer, airspeed unreliable, etc.). These live in the AA QRH/QRC, not in the AOM. They are PROCEDURES, not limitations.

This master document covers AOM Memorized Limitations exhaustively. For non-normal memory items, refer to the QRH/QRC and the per-checklist Memory Items sections in those documents.

## Quick Reference — Hard Numbers Cheat Sheet

**Speeds**
- VMO / MMO: 340 kt / .82 M
- Turbulence: 280 kt / .76 M
- VLO ext: 270 / .82, VLE: 320 / .82, VLO ret: 235
- Flap placards: 240/240/240/210/200/190/175/162-166

**Altitudes**
- Max op: 41,000 ft
- Max T/O & Land: 8,400 ft
- Max with flaps/slats: 20,000 ft
- Unpressurized inflight: 14,000 ft (terrain exceptions)
- Unpressurized dispatch: 10,000 ft
- 250 KIAS rule: below 10,000 ft

**Weights (NG / MAX)**
- Taxi: 174,700 / 182,700
- T/O: 174,200 / 182,200
- Land: 146,300 / 152,800
- ZFW: 138,300 / 145,400

**Wind**
- Max wind T/O & Land: 50 kt
- T/O tailwind: 15 (special data)
- Land tailwind: 10
- Headwind CAT II/III: 25
- HUD T/O tailwind: 10

**Autopilot**
- Min after T/O: 400 ft AGL
- Min single-channel approach: 50 ft AGL
- Below 1000 ft AFE on final: no LVL CHG
- Aileron trim with AP: prohibited

**APU altitudes**
- Bleed + electrical: 10,000 ft
- Bleed: 17,000 ft
- Electrical: 41,000 ft
- 3 aborted starts → 15 min cooldown

**Speedbrakes**
- No deploy in flight < 1000 ft AFL (RA)
- Inflight: do not exceed FLIGHT detent
- MAX 8 with flaps 40: do not exceed ARMED detent

**Reverse**
- Ground only; in-flight intentional use prohibited
- No GA after reverser deployment on landing

**Icing**
- Definition: OAT/TAT ≤ 10°C + visible moisture (or surface contam on ground)
- EAI: ON in icing (exception cruise/climb < -40°C SAT)
- WAI: not on ground above 10°C; not a substitute for deice
- Holding with flaps in icing: prohibited

**Cabin / Pressure**
- Max diff (relief): 9.1 psi
- Unpressurized post-event: 14,000 ft

**Stabilized approach gate**
- 1000 ft AFL IMC and VMC: gear, landing flaps, ≤ 1000 fpm
- 1000 ft IMC speed/path/thrust (or 500 ft VMC if called)
- < 2000 AFL: ≤ 2000 fpm; < 1000 AFL or inside FAF: ≤ 1000 fpm

**Stall / Upset / WS / EGPWS pitch targets**
- Stall recovery: nose-down to break shaker; FD ignored
- Upset nose-high recovery: roll up to ~60° to get pitch rate
- Windshear: 15° initial pitch
- EGPWS pull-up: 20° initial pitch
- All three: AP/AT off, MAX thrust as appropriate, do NOT change gear/flap config until clear

**TCAS**
- DESCEND RA < 1000 ft AGL: NOT followed
- RA vertical takes precedence over ATC vertical (comply ATC lateral)
- Climb RA landing config: max thrust → flaps 15 → gear up
